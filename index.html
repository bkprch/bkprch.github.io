Free retro-style games for Roku

Free, family-friendly games for your Roku. Simple to play with the regular remote, made by a small family team, and tested on our own TV every evening.

The games

Floe Hopper

Hop across drifting ice to build your igloo before you freeze.

Gem Drop

Clear the ice with matches, line blasters and color bombs.

Haunted Manor

Creep through a pitch-black house hunting for the lost relic.

Castle Siege

Pass the remote! Take turns lobbing boulders at each other's castles.

Jungle Joe

Swing on vines and hop crocodiles to find all 8 treasures in time.

Doubles

Slide and merge matching numbers. Can you make a 2048 tile?

Garden Snake

Eat apples, grow longer, and don't bite your own tail!

Brickyard

Bust through brick, concrete and steel walls. Catch the power-ups!

Sunset Speedway

Race 3 laps at sundown before the clock runs out. Pass cars for points!

The Last Seed

Wasteland quest: find the last seed and carry it home. Beware the robots!

Nest Buster

A firefly versus the Wasp Queen! Bite through the comb and zap her.

Henhouse Hustle

Catch Dottie's eggs before they splat! Three misses and you're done.

Solitaire

Rocking Chair Solitaire: the classic seven-column card game. Rewind undoes, FF hints.

River Hawk

Fly a floatplane up the river - it fires on its own. Watch your fuel!

Three Hills

Three Hills Solitaire: play cards one higher or lower to clear three hills. Build long runs!

Clothesline

Clothesline Solitaire: every card face up, four clothespins to hang cards on. Nearly every deal can be won!

Hayloft Havoc

Slide hay bales to bowl over the raccoons raiding the barn. Line up the golden bales!

Stump Jump

Hop Pip the tree frog down a pyramid of stumps, turning each one mossy green. Watch out for the crow!
